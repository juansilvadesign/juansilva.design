## Format & Timing Breakdown (15.0s = 900 frames)

- **0.0s – 3.0s**: Settled opening on the calculator interface. Input values: Session `R$ 250`, Inquiries `2/mo`, Sessions `4/mo`. Base curve: `R$ 42.000` without process vs `R$ 105.000` with process.
- **3.0s – 7.5s**: Animated cursor adjusts session price (`R$ 250` → `R$ 300`) and new patients per month (`5` → `8`). Curves dynamically bend and diverge on the cohort graph.
- **7.5s – 11.5s**: Callout card highlights the recalculated difference: `R$ 63.000` → `R$ 96.000` lost revenue. Luminous green pulse around the difference metric.
- **11.5s – 15.0s**: Inputs and graph smoothly interpolate back to exact baseline state (Frame 0). Frame 900 matches Frame 0 pixel-for-pixel. Seamless loop.
